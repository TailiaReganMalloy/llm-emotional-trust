\subsubsection{Education-Based Trust-Change Comparison Condition-Effect Results}
Condition-effect tests (Interactive vs Text) were run within each Education category for analytical change, emotional change, and analytical-minus-emotional difference.

Analytical Change (Post - Pre, per-item) (min n per condition = 8):
- High School: $n_{Interactive}=45$, $n_{Text}=59$, $M_{Interactive}=0.089$, $M_{Text}=0.024$, $t=0.547$, $p = .586$, $\Delta(M_I-M_T)=0.065$, ns.
- Bachelor: $n_{Interactive}=120$, $n_{Text}=121$, $M_{Interactive}=-0.225$, $M_{Text}=-0.191$, $t=-0.442$, $p = .659$, $\Delta(M_I-M_T)=-0.034$, ns.
- Master: $n_{Interactive}=55$, $n_{Text}=43$, $M_{Interactive}=-0.122$, $M_{Text}=-0.191$, $t=0.540$, $p = .590$, $\Delta(M_I-M_T)=0.069$, ns.
- Graduate Professional Degree: $n_{Interactive}=24$, $n_{Text}=21$, $M_{Interactive}=-0.250$, $M_{Text}=-0.076$, $t=-0.799$, $p = .429$, $\Delta(M_I-M_T)=-0.174$, ns.
- PhD: $n_{Interactive}=8$, $n_{Text}=8$, $M_{Interactive}=-0.012$, $M_{Text}=0.038$, $t=-0.190$, $p = .852$, $\Delta(M_I-M_T)=-0.050$, ns.
