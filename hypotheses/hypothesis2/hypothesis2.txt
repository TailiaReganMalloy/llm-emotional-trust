2.1 WEIRD Overall pre-post: $n=120$, $M_{pre}=1.217$, $M_{post}=-1.133$, $t(119)=3.440$, $p < .001$, $W=1848.000$, $p_{W} < .001$, $d=-0.314$, $\Delta=-2.350$.
