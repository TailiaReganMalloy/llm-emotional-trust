\section*{Exploratory Sentiment Figures}
This section summarizes the two exploratory sentiment plots with explicit significance status for each main test.

\subsection*{Figure 1: Sentiment by Group and Condition}
Figure 1 compares participant-level mean sentiment scores across WEIRD and NORMAL groups and across Interactive and Text conditions.
\begin{itemize}
\item Overall condition contrast (Interactive vs Text): ns (\(p = .979\)).
\item Overall group contrast (WEIRD vs NORMAL): ns (\(p = .213\)).
\item Condition-by-group interaction (OLS interaction term): ns (\(p = .881\)).
\item Within-group condition contrasts: WEIRD = ns, NORMAL = ns.
\item Within-condition group contrasts: Interactive = ns, Text = ns.
\end{itemize}
\begin{figure}[h]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.9\linewidth]{exploratory_qualitative_analysis/results/sentiment_by_group_condition.png}
\caption{Open-response sentiment by group and condition with explicit significance labels (stars or ns).}
\label{fig:exploratory_sentiment_group_condition}
\end{figure}

\subsection*{Figure 2: Sentiment Associations With Trust Change}
Figure 2 shows sentiment-to-trust-change associations with panel-specific significance labeling for all-participant and subgroup/condition correlations.
\begin{itemize}
\item Overall trust change: ns (\(p = .414\)) in the all-sample Pearson test.
\item Analytical trust change: ** (\(p = .006\)) in the all-sample Pearson test.
\item Emotional trust change: * (\(p = .011\)) in the all-sample Pearson test.
\item Analytical minus emotional trust change: *** (\(p < .001\)) in the all-sample Pearson test.
\end{itemize}
\begin{figure}[h]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.92\linewidth]{exploratory_qualitative_analysis/results/sentiment_vs_trust_changes.png}
\caption{Sentiment versus trust-change outcomes with significance annotations (stars or ns) per panel.}
\label{fig:exploratory_sentiment_trust_changes}
\end{figure}

\noindent Significance key: \(* p<.05\), \(** p<.01\), \(*** p<.001\), ns = not significant.
