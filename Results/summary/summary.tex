\noindent For analytical trust, non-WEIRD participants scored higher than WEIRD participants ($M=0.28$ vs. $M=0.01$; Welch $t=-2.95$, two-sided $p = .0034$, one-sided $p = .0017$). For emotional trust, non-WEIRD participants scored higher than WEIRD participants ($M=0.35$ vs. $M=0.19$; Welch $t=-3.38$, two-sided $p = .0008$, one-sided $p = .0004$). For overall trust, non-WEIRD participants scored higher than WEIRD participants ($M=0.32$ vs. $M=0.10$; Welch $t=-3.46$, two-sided $p = .0006$, one-sided $p = .0003$).

\noindent No condition difference was detected for analytical post when averaging across participants (Welch $t=0.15$, two-sided $p = .8814$). No condition difference was detected for analytical change when averaging across participants (Welch $t=-0.33$, two-sided $p = .7393$). No condition difference was detected for emotional post when averaging across participants (Welch $t=1.85$, two-sided $p = .0649$). No condition difference was detected for emotional change when averaging across participants (Welch $t=0.41$, two-sided $p = .6802$). No condition difference was detected for overall post when averaging across participants (Welch $t=0.34$, two-sided $p = .7344$). No condition difference was detected for overall change when averaging across participants (Welch $t=-0.02$, two-sided $p = .9837$).

\noindent For analytical trust post, Interactive non-WEIRD was higher than Interactive WEIRD (Welch $t=-2.97$, two-sided $p = .0036$, one-sided $p = .0018$); Text non-WEIRD was higher than Interactive WEIRD (Welch $t=-2.27$, two-sided $p = .0245$, one-sided $p = .0123$). No pairwise group differences reached significance for analytical trust change. For emotional trust post, Interactive non-WEIRD was higher than Text non-WEIRD (Welch $t=2.59$, two-sided $p = .0100$, one-sided $p = .0050$); Text WEIRD was higher than Text non-WEIRD (Welch $t=2.45$, two-sided $p = .0152$, one-sided $p = .0076$). For emotional trust change, Interactive WEIRD was higher than Text non-WEIRD (Welch $t=2.51$, two-sided $p = .0135$, one-sided $p = .0067$); Text WEIRD was higher than Interactive non-WEIRD (Welch $t=-2.92$, two-sided $p = .0041$, one-sided $p = .0020$); Text WEIRD was higher than Text non-WEIRD (Welch $t=4.28$, two-sided $p < .0001$, one-sided $p < .0001$). For overall trust post, Interactive non-WEIRD was higher than Interactive WEIRD (Welch $t=-2.91$, two-sided $p = .0043$, one-sided $p = .0021$); Text non-WEIRD was higher than Interactive WEIRD (Welch $t=-2.01$, two-sided $p = .0467$, one-sided $p = .0233$). For overall trust change, Text WEIRD was higher than Interactive non-WEIRD (Welch $t=-2.40$, two-sided $p = .0174$, one-sided $p = .0087$); Text WEIRD was higher than Text non-WEIRD (Welch $t=3.36$, two-sided $p = .0009$, one-sided $p = .0005$).
