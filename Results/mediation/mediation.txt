\subsubsection{Mediation Of WEIRD Attributes Across Conditions}
Across both conditions combined (Interactive and Text), separate mediation models tested whether each WEIRD-defining attribute mediated the association between WEIRD group membership and the analytical-vs-emotional change difference.
Model per test: X = WEIRD indicator, M = attribute indicator, Y = analytical_change - emotional_change; indirect effect estimated by non-parametric bootstrap.

For the western geographic indicator (residence or origin) as a mediator, analysis of (n=454) showed an a path (X->M) of 0.6819, a b path (M->Y | X) of -0.0250, a c total path (X->Y) of -0.0770, and a c' direct path (X->Y | M) of -0.0599.
The estimated indirect effect (a*b) was -0.0168, with a 95% CI of [-0.1019, 0.0672] and p = .686.
This indicates no clear evidence of mediation because the bootstrap confidence interval includes zero.

The estimated indirect effect (a*b) was -0.0509, with a 95% CI of [-0.1091, 0.0050] and p = .073.
This indicates no clear evidence of mediation because the bootstrap confidence interval includes zero.
