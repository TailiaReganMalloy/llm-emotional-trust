\subsection{Hypothesis 2}
\begin{center}
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{Figures/hypothesis2.png}
\vspace{-20pt}
\captionof{figure}{Visualization for Hypothesis 2.}
\label{fig:hypothesis2}
\end{center}

Hypothesis 2 tested whether experimental condition changed the magnitude of trust change across analytical, emotional, and overall trust outcomes. Figure \ref{fig:hypothesis2} summarizes the mean change scores for the Interactive and Static conditions, and the primary analyses used Welch's two-sample t-tests to compare conditions for each outcome.

Across $n_{Interactive}=223$ and $n_{Text}=231$ participants, analytical trust change was $M_{Interactive}=-0.155$ (95\% CI [-0.237, -0.073]) in the Interactive condition and $M_{Text}=-0.101$ (95\% CI [-0.175, -0.028]) in the Static condition, Welch's $t(444.81)=-0.967$, $p = .334$, and $\Delta(M_I-M_T)=-0.054$. Sub-hypothesis 2.1 was not statistically significant.

Emotional trust change followed the same pattern: $M_{Interactive}=-0.067$ (95\% CI [-0.120, -0.013]) for Interactive participants and $M_{Text}=-0.038$ (95\% CI [-0.086, 0.009]) for Static participants, Welch's $t(444.20)=-0.776$, $p = .438$, and $\Delta(M_I-M_T)=-0.028$. Sub-hypothesis 2.2 was not statistically significant.

Overall trust change also did not differ meaningfully by condition, with $M_{Interactive}=-0.111$ (95\% CI [-0.167, -0.055]) in the Interactive condition and $M_{Text}=-0.070$ (95\% CI [-0.120, -0.020]) in the Static condition, Welch's $t(444.21)=-1.075$, $p = .283$, and $\Delta(M_I-M_T)=-0.041$. Sub-hypothesis 2.3 was not statistically significant. Taken together, these results indicate that the Interactive and Static conditions produced comparable changes in trust, so Hypothesis 2 was not supported.

\textbf{Benjamini-Hochberg Procedure:} Using the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure at false discovery rate $q=0.05$ for the three primary sub-hypothesis tests, sub-hypothesis 2.1 was not statistically significant, sub-hypothesis 2.2 was not statistically significant, and sub-hypothesis 2.3 was not statistically significant.
