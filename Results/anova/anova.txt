\subsection{ANOVA: Condition and Demographic Effects}
A two-way ANOVA tested main effects of condition (Interactive vs. Text), demographic group (WEIRD vs. NORMAL), and their interaction for trust-change outcomes.
Sample: $n=454$ ($n_{WEIRD}=150$, $n_{NORMAL}=304$).

Overall Trust Change
Condition main effect: $F(1, 450)=1.192$, $p = .275$, $\eta_p^2=0.003$, not significant.
Demographic main effect (WEIRD vs NORMAL): $F(1, 450)=0.042$, $p = .837$, $\eta_p^2=0.000$, not significant.
Condition x Demographic interaction: $F(1, 450)=2.359$, $p = .125$, $\eta_p^2=0.005$, not significant.

Emotional Trust Change
Condition main effect: $F(1, 450)=0.650$, $p = .420$, $\eta_p^2=0.001$, not significant.
Demographic main effect (WEIRD vs NORMAL): $F(1, 450)=0.115$, $p = .735$, $\eta_p^2=0.000$, not significant.
Condition x Demographic interaction: $F(1, 450)=3.892$, $p = .049$, $\eta_p^2=0.009$, significant.

Analytical Trust Change
Condition main effect: $F(1, 450)=0.938$, $p = .333$, $\eta_p^2=0.002$, not significant.
Demographic main effect (WEIRD vs NORMAL): $F(1, 450)=0.004$, $p = .952$, $\eta_p^2=0.000$, not significant.
Condition x Demographic interaction: $F(1, 450)=0.665$, $p = .415$, $\eta_p^2=0.001$, not significant.

Interpretation summary:
Significant ANOVA effects were detected for: Emotional interaction.
