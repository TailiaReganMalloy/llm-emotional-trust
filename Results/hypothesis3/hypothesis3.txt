\subsection{Hypothesis 3}
\begin{center}
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{Figures/hypothesis3.png}
\vspace{-20pt}
\captionof{figure}{Visualization for Hypothesis 3.}
\label{fig:hypothesis3}
\end{center}

Hypothesis 3 tested whether WEIRD participants showed a distinct trust-change pattern across analytical, emotional, and overall trust. Figure \ref{fig:hypothesis3} summarizes these three pre-post tests.

Across $150$ WEIRD participants, analytical trust decreased from $M_{pre}=0.025$ (95\% CI [-0.127, 0.178]) to $M_{post}=-0.101$ (95\% CI [-0.252, 0.050]), $t(149)=2.860$, $p = .005$, $W=2847.500$, $p_{W} = .010$, $d=-0.234$, and $\Delta=-0.127$. Emotional trust changed from $M_{pre}=0.187$ (95\% CI [0.106, 0.267]) to $M_{post}=0.127$ (95\% CI [0.036, 0.219]), $t(149)=1.749$, $p = .082$, $W=1541.000$, $p_{W} = .057$, $d=-0.143$, and $\Delta=-0.059$.

Overall trust decreased from $M_{pre}=0.106$ (95\% CI [0.001, 0.211]) to $M_{post}=0.013$ (95\% CI [-0.098, 0.124]), $t(149)=2.989$, $p = .003$, $W=3606.000$, $p_{W} = .008$, $d=-0.244$, and $\Delta=-0.093$. Sub-hypothesis 3.1 was statistically significant, sub-hypothesis 3.2 was not statistically significant, and sub-hypothesis 3.3 was statistically significant.

\textbf{Benjamini-Hochberg Procedure:} Using the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure at false discovery rate $q=0.05$ for the three primary sub-hypothesis tests, sub-hypothesis 3.1 was statistically significant, sub-hypothesis 3.2 was not statistically significant, and sub-hypothesis 3.3 was statistically significant.
