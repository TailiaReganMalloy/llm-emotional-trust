\subsection{Hypothesis 1}
\begin{center}
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{Figures/hypothesis1.png}
\vspace{-20pt}
\captionof{figure}{Visualization for Hypothesis 1.}
\label{fig:hypothesis1}
\end{center}

Hypothesis 1 tested whether trust changed from pre- to post-experiment across analytical, emotional, and overall trust dimensions. Figure \ref{fig:hypothesis1} summarizes the pre-post means, and all analyses used paired-samples t-tests with two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests as a robustness check.

Across $454$ paired responses, analytical trust decreased from $M_{pre}=0.205$ (95\% CI [0.119, 0.292]) to $M_{post}=0.078$ (95\% CI [-0.008, 0.163]), $t(453)=4.597$, $p < .001$, $W=28342.000$, $p_{W} < .001$, $d=-0.216$, and $\Delta=-0.128$. Emotional trust also declined from $M_{pre}=0.289$ (95\% CI [0.244, 0.333]) to $M_{post}=0.236$ (95\% CI [0.185, 0.287]), $t(453)=2.879$, $p = .004$, $W=14885.000$, $p_{W} = .007$, $d=-0.135$, and $\Delta=-0.052$.

Overall trust decreased from $M_{pre}=0.247$ (95\% CI [0.188, 0.306]) to $M_{post}=0.157$ (95\% CI [0.095, 0.219]), $t(453)=4.724$, $p < .001$, $W=34761.500$, $p_{W} < .001$, $d=-0.222$, and $\Delta=-0.090$. Sub-hypothesis 1.1 was statistically significant, sub-hypothesis 1.2 was statistically significant, and sub-hypothesis 1.3 was statistically significant, so all three components support Hypothesis 1.

\textbf{Benjamini-Hochberg Procedure:} Using the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure at false discovery rate $q=0.05$ for the three primary sub-hypothesis tests, sub-hypothesis 1.1 was statistically significant, sub-hypothesis 1.2 was statistically significant, and sub-hypothesis 1.3 was statistically significant.
