\subsubsection{Hypothesis 3 Results}
Hypothesis 3 tested NORMAL/non-WEIRD participants with four subtests: 3.1 overall pre-post significance, 3.2 analytical pre-post significance, 3.3 emotional pre-post significance, and 3.4 whether emotional and analytical standardized change do not differ.

3.1 NORMAL Overall pre-post: $n=347$, $M_{pre}=0.318$, $M_{post}=0.089$, $t(346)=10.093$, $p < .001$, $W=13019.000$, $p_{W} < .001$, $d=-0.542$, $\Delta=-0.229$.
3.2 NORMAL Analytical pre-post: $n=347$, $M_{pre}=0.283$, $M_{post}=0.139$, $t(346)=4.151$, $p < .001$, $W=17408.500$, $p_{W} = .001$, $d=-0.223$, $\Delta=-0.144$.
3.3 NORMAL Emotional pre-post: $n=347$, $M_{pre}=0.354$, $M_{post}=0.040$, $t(346)=13.386$, $p < .001$, $W=5349.000$, $p_{W} < .001$, $d=-0.719$, $\Delta=-0.314$.
3.4 NORMAL emotional-vs-analytical standardized change: $n=347$, $M_{em,z}=-0.119$, $M_{an,z}=-0.018$, $t(346)=-1.470$, $p = .143$, $W=26879.000$, $p_{W} = .077$, $d=-0.079$, $\Delta_{z}=-0.101$. This supports the predicted no-difference test.
